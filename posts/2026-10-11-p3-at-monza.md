Season two of Sector 04 Racing got going at Monza, and it started the way I wanted: second on the grid and third at the flag.

It's my first season in the McLaren seat. Last season I only joined for the final two rounds, straight in with no practice, so this is the first time I've started a Sector 04 season from round one.

- Qualified P2
- Finished P3
- 17 points

Next up is a wildcard round at Montréal.
