How I drive

Aggressive on entry, controlled on the pass, and strongest off the line.

Aggressive, not reckless

I'm on the aggressive side and always pushing to find more time. When it comes to the overtake I'm controlled: the move gets set up, not thrown.

Starts are my strength

The launch is the phase I'm most confident in. A clean getaway and places made up into turn one is where I gain the most. Test your start against mine.

Keep your nose clean

You can't always win it on the first lap. Keep your nose clean, stay out of other people's accidents, and the result comes to you.

The rival is my teammate

No bad blood, but the driver in the same car is the first person I'm trying to beat every weekend.
