  1. GridStart line

    Lights out

    I'm Z0MB1XCAT, an open-wheel sim racer from Swansea in Wales. This is how I got here, told one corner at a time around the first circuit I ever learned. Keep scrolling to drive.

  2. 1Niki Lauda

    The birthday

    On my birthday I walked into Simrace1, the sim racing centre in Swansea, for the first time. I was hooked before I'd properly sat down.

  3. 2The kink

    That Christmas

    That Christmas I had a rig of my own. No more booking slots: I could practise whenever I wanted, and the laps started piling up.

  4. 3Remus

    First league race

    My first ever league race was in Chaos Grid, at Bahrain, and it lived up to the name. Cars everywhere, all race long. I kept it out of the wreckage and brought it home seventh.

    P7BahrainChaos Grid

  5. 4Schlossgold

    The lesson

    You can't always win it on the first lap.

    Keep your nose clean, stay out of other people's accidents, and the result comes to you.

  6. 5Into the infield

    Three leagues, three teams

    AlpineSee The Air
    McLarenSector 04 Racing
    WilliamsOverdrive Racing Series

    Keeping on top of the chaos across all three is half the fun.

  7. 6Long left

    The rival

    My biggest rival is always my teammate. No bad blood, but the driver in the same car is the first person I'm trying to beat every weekend.

  8. 7Back on the power

    On the podium

    8 podiums so far. Three of my favourites:

    P2CanadaChaos Grid
    P3SuzukaSee The Air, Alpine
    P3MonzaSector 04 Racing, McLaren

  9. 8Downhill

    Six hours, solo

    I race endurance events with Mackles Racing. My longest race so far was six hours at Road America in an LMP2, driven solo from start to finish.

    Mackles Racing on SimGrid

  10. 9Fast right

    Starts are my strength

    I'm aggressive on entry and controlled on the pass, but the launch is where I'm most confident: a clean getaway and places made up into turn one. My best reaction is 0.183 seconds.

    Try to beat it

  11. 10Onto the straight

    Racing for Wales

    My proudest moment isn't a result. It's lining up on every grid as a Welsh driver.

  12. FlagFinish line

    Still chasing P1

    No win yet, and I want one badly. Next, I want to race in the PSGL and get scouted, join One Hub Racing, and take this as far as it goes, in sim racing or in a real car.
