The spec

What each part is, and why it matters when you're chasing a lap time.

  1. 1

    WheelbaseMoza R9 V3

    Direct drive. The detail coming through the wheel is what makes catching a slide possible rather than lucky.

  2. 2

    WheelMoza GS V2P

    A formula-style rim, with everything I need mapped to the front so my hands stay where they should.

  3. 3

    PedalsMoza CRP2

    Loadcell brake. You brake by pressure rather than travel, which is where the consistency comes from.

  4. 4

    CockpitGT Omega Prime Lite

    An aluminium profile frame with no flex under load, which matters a lot with a direct drive base bolted to it.

  5. 5

    SeatGT Omega Inertia

    A bucket seat that holds you in place, so you're not bracing against the wheel through a long stint.

  6. 6

    Display32" 1440p, 180 Hz

    A single curved 32 inch panel. High refresh matters far more than resolution for judging a braking point.
